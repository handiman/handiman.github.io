Today I launched semantic.cv — a small idea that turned into something I’ve wanted for a long time.

It’s a structured, JSON‑LD–based way to write and publish a CV with clean, minimal themes. There have been earlier attempts at structured CV formats, but none that fully embraced modern standards or offered an end‑to‑end workflow. semantic.cv is my take on that idea — simple, deterministic, and built for 2026.

I updated my own CV with it, and it feels good to ship something that’s both useful and personal. A few weeks of focused work, a lot of iteration, and a product I’m proud to put my name on.

If you’re curious, it’s live here: [https://semantic.cv](https://semantic.cv)
