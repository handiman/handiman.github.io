The inevitable JavaScript alerts? The default background color? Blink? Marquee? Webrings?

And how about being able to show your e-mail address to the world without running the risk of :flame: or an army of :trollface: coming your way?

Now you can have all of that implemented as web components and make your place on the internet beautifully ugly: [https://github.com/handiman/retro](https://github.com/handiman/retro). Take whatever you don't want :wink:
