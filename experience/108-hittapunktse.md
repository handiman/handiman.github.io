## Context
I joined hitta.se during a period of fast growth. At the time it was one of Sweden's ten most visited websites.

## Work
### Public services
- Designed and built **hitta.se/trafiknytt**, using traffic data from the Swedish road authority (**Vägverket** at the time)
- Built **hitta.se/vader**, bringing weather data from **Storm.no** into the map
- New features and performance work on the public map site
### Internal systems
- Maintained and extended the internal order system
- ASP.NET, C#, T-SQL, MSMQ, XML/XSLT and IIS
### Frontend
- Client-side work with AJAX, JavaScript, XHTML and CSS
