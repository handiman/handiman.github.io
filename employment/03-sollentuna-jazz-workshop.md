## About the role
I stood in for one of the regular study circle leaders at Sollentuna Jazz Workshop, a community programme for ensemble playing and jazz education. It was connected to my own music studies at the time.

## What I did
- Led rehearsals and helped participants with ensemble playing, timing and improvisation
