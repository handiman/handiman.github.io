## About the role
I joined Betsson as a consultant and later moved into a permanent role as Senior Software Engineer, in the Player Rewards & Gamification value stream. I worked on three services: **Tournaments**, where I spent most of my time, **Loyalty**, a legacy system, and **Levels**, the newer service meant to replace it.

## What I did
### Tournaments
Players opt into leaderboard competitions where points are awarded by configurable rules, for example Y points for winning X euro on a slot machine. Casino tournaments were already in place when I joined, and I extended the system to Sportsbook.

- Implemented point awarding for different event types
- Extended the Casino tournament system to handle Sportsbook betting events
- Found that excessive logging caused most of the performance problems in the Kafka pipeline, and removed it
- Made the scoring and event-processing code clearer

### Levels
Levels was the planned successor to Loyalty, with achievements, progression and more flexible gamification.

- Domain modelling of the core concepts
- Proofs of concept for architectural and functional ideas
- Introduced [.NET Aspire](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/aspire/) for local development and integration testing
- Designed the PostgreSQL data model and containerized the application with Docker

### Loyalty
- Maintenance and smaller improvements

## Why I left
I enjoyed my time at Betsson and the people I worked with. I left only because I missed being my own boss.
