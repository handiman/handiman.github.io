## About the role
I worked as a shop assistant for Konsumentföreningen Stockholm through my late teens and early twenties, mostly at a store on Lidingö, with periods in Nacka and a shorter stint at Rådhuset in central Stockholm. It paid my way until I moved into IT.

## What I did
- Stocking, checkout and customer service
- Responsible for the greens section on Lidingö and the beverages section in Nacka
- Opened and closed the stores and made sure the day's takings went into the store deposits
