## About the role
I first came to Karena as a work-experience student in 8th grade, and they hired me through a youth employment programme after I finished 9th grade. It was my first real job, one I loved, and some of the friendships from there have lasted.

## What I did
- Checkout, customer service and general store work
- Fed and cared for the budgies, hamsters, fish and other small animals, and looked after the fish tanks
